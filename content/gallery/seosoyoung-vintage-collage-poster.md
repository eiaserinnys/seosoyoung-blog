```text
[Customize]
Brand: PAPERROOM
Theme: Books / Independent Bookshop & Print Culture
Portrait: A young East Asian woman in her early 20s preserving the exact facial identity, eye shape, nose, lips, and serene intellectual expression of the reference image, keeping the reference subject's hair and dark spherical hair ornaments as in the seed
Collage Elements: torn antique book pages, woodblock botanical engravings, library due-date cards stamped with 'A QUIETER BRIGHTER WORLD', archival reading room photographs, gilt-lettered vintage book spines ('THE READING ROOM', 'FIRST EDITION', 'PAPER ARCHIVE'), fountain pen nib illustrations, and handwritten margin notes
Visual Twist: an open vintage hardcover book with fanning cream pages blooming seamlessly out of the upper crown of her dark hair, opening into a warm miniature candlelit library reading room nestled inside the spread
Colors: Color palette 'Forest Library' (087): warm parchment cream #EDE6D4 and aged book-page tan #C4B38E shaping the sculptural planes of the face under 45-degree key light, muted botanical sage #8D9470 and bookshop green #526C58 in surrounding cutouts, deep forest green #293F38 framing the portrait and forming the solid bottom branding panel, with selective vintage crimson stamp accents #B4594F
Tagline: MADE OF STORIES.
Details: INDEPENDENT BOOKSHOP & PRINT CULTURE / CLOISTERMONT / BOOKS / PRINT / IDEAS

Style & Composition

Create a stunning 9:16 vintage editorial collage poster featuring a giant human portrait made entirely from torn magazines, archival photographs, printed text, illustrations, and layered paper fragments.

The face should remain clearly recognizable, with expressive eyes and natural proportions. Build facial features through overlapping paper shapes and tonal contrast, rather than simply overlaying textures on a photograph.

Creative Integration

Seamlessly blend an open vintage hardcover book with fanning cream pages blooming seamlessly out of the upper crown of her dark hair, opening into a warm miniature candlelit library reading room nestled inside the spread into the face, hair, or surrounding collage. The interaction should feel surprising yet physically believable, creating a strong visual focal point.

Collage & Texture

Fill the composition with rich, theme-related torn antique book pages, woodblock botanical engravings, library due-date cards stamped with 'A QUIETER BRIGHTER WORLD', archival reading room photographs, gilt-lettered vintage book spines ('THE READING ROOM', 'FIRST EDITION', 'PAPER ARCHIVE'), fountain pen nib illustrations, and handwritten margin notes. Mix vintage typography, photography, handwritten notes, and irregular paper cutouts at different scales and angles.

Emphasize authentic handmade textures: torn edges, paper fibers, print halftones, subtle folds, and realistic overlapping shadows.

Color & Layout

Use Color palette 'Forest Library' (087): warm parchment cream #EDE6D4 and aged book-page tan #C4B38E shaping the sculptural planes of the face under 45-degree key light, muted botanical sage #8D9470 and bookshop green #526C58 in surrounding cutouts, deep forest green #293F38 framing the portrait and forming the solid bottom branding panel, with selective vintage crimson stamp accents #B4594F to create contrast: lighter tones shaping the face, darker fragments framing it, and selective vibrant accents drawing attention.

Maintain high visual density while keeping the portrait dominant and recognizable.

Branding

Reserve the bottom 12-15% for a solid-color branding panel featuring PAPERROOM, MADE OF STORIES., and INDEPENDENT BOOKSHOP & PRINT CULTURE / CLOISTERMONT / BOOKS / PRINT / IDEAS.

Use clean, elegant typography with accurate spelling and professional spacing.

Final Look

A sophisticated, gallery-quality analog collage with intricate editorial details, tactile paper textures, and strong commercial poster aesthetics.

9:16 vertical, high resolution, full-bleed composition.
```
